# Choice Audit Checklist

## Before choice
- Decision question clear?
- Stakes visible?
- Relevant information available?
- Commitment level signaled?

## Options
- Different intentions?
- Different tradeoffs?
- Dominant option?
- Redundant option?

## After choice
- Immediate reaction?
- Persistent state?
- Later callback?
- Consequence legible?

## Production
- Unique scenes?
- VO variants?
- Quest variants?
- Could state changes preserve agency with less branch cost?
