Tryb: wbudowany image_gen, nowa ilustracja. Ramka i polskie podpisy są składane osobno przez generator PDF.

Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: black-and-white illustration for a physical fantasy board-game terrain tile, printed only 48 mm wide by 16 mm tall.
Primary request: one clearly recognizable medieval wooden four-wheeled freight wagon, empty and ready to carry a heavy bell. Depict its real construction: broad open plank cargo bed, low plank sides, strong undercarriage, two axles, large spoked wooden wheels with iron rims, and two wooden tow shafts projecting towards the right. Consistent physically plausible construction and perspective.
Composition: extremely wide landscape image, 3:1 aspect ratio. Wagon in a shallow three-quarter side view slightly from above, front pointing right, allowing us to see inside its empty bed. The silhouette including shafts should fill almost the whole width, with small safe white margins and no cropping. Wagon body and wheels must remain large and unmistakable, not a tiny pictogram surrounded by white.
Style: professional fantasy tabletop rulebook pen-and-ink illustration. Concrete, convincing, slightly hand-drawn craftsmanship, confident thick outer contour and simpler medium-weight interior lines. Show just enough plank grain, iron bands and wheel spokes to read well at tiny print size. Minimal restrained hatching; mostly open white surfaces. More representational than an abstract schematic icon, simpler and clearer than an intricate etching.
Palette/background: pure black ink on clean pure white. No color, no grayscale washes, no gradients, no solid black shadows, no paper texture, no ground scenery or cast shadow. No people, horses, mule, bell or cargo.
Text: none. No border, no lettering, no numbers, no grid. The frame and Polish mechanics will be typeset separately. Output only the illustration.
